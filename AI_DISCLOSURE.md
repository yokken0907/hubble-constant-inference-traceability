# AI Assistance Disclosure

## Scope of AI assistance

Generative AI was used extensively as a research tool in this project.

**Primary general-purpose AI research system (non-author):** OpenAI ChatGPT (GPT-5.6 Thinking)

ChatGPT assisted with organizing public materials, research framing, candidate methodology, code drafting, cross-source synthesis, numerical interpretation, evidence mapping, bounded-claim formulation, documentation, and manuscript preparation.

**Supporting coding and repository system (non-author):** OpenAI Codex

Codex supported code-generation, repository inspection, metadata maintenance, and packaging-related tasks.

Both systems are non-author tools. AI output itself was not treated as scientific evidence. Scientific claims are tied to cited public sources, archived machine-readable outputs, frozen registers, documented corrections, and explicit claim boundaries.

## Human contribution and accountability

Keiji Yoshimura selected the research subject and questions; approved the scope and claim boundaries; directed and operated the computational workflow; acquired, organized, and preserved source and result files; requested reruns, corrections, and stopping when needed; maintained provenance and integrity records; and made final release and publication decisions.

The human author's role does not imply unaided domain-expert derivation of every technical component. It identifies the decisions and continuing responsibilities actually exercised in the project. The human author retains responsibility for correcting or withdrawing public claims if later evidence shows them to be unsupported.

## Verification boundary

AI-assisted material was checked against archived machine-readable outputs, SHA-256 records, public-source records, documented corrections, and tests designed to detect overstatement. These checks support traceability and bounded verification; they do not substitute for independent domain-expert review or external independent replication.

Independent expert review remains pending. This disclosure is intended to describe the division of labor accurately, without presenting the work as either human-only or AI-autonomous research.
