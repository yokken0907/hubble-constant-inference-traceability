# AI Assistance Disclosure

This project was conducted by Keiji Yoshimura, a non-specialist independent researcher, with extensive assistance from general-purpose artificial-intelligence systems.

AI assistance included, depending on the project stage:

- drafting and revising analysis scripts;
- organizing public-source and provenance records;
- proposing numerical and integrity checks;
- reviewing intermediate outputs and documented limitations;
- drafting tables, explanatory text, and repository documentation; and
- helping identify inconsistencies requiring correction or claim restriction.

The human author selected the research questions, supplied or approved the source material, executed or commissioned the computational steps, reviewed outputs, decided what to retain or reject, and accepts responsibility for the published claims and errors.

AI output is not treated as an authority, an independent scientific replication, or expert review. Machine-generated suggestions were required to remain subordinate to recorded source material, executable outputs, checksums, and explicit claim boundaries. The repository nevertheless may contain mistakes that neither the author nor the assisting systems detected.

Readers should evaluate the manuscript and repository from the underlying evidence and reproducible records rather than from the fact that AI assistance was used.
